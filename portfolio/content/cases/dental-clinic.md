# Client Problem
Aesthetic Care Dental is a high-end clinic specializing in anxiety-free dental implants and cosmetic treatments. They struggled with the clinical, intimidating look of typical dental ads which caused high bounce rates on their booking page. They needed a disarming, comforting, and highly approachable animation that demystified the process and made dentistry feel cozy.

# Creative Idea
We developed a whimsical, soft-claymation animated universe where teeth aren't scary and procedures are represented as gentle, bubbly cleansings. We avoided realistic medical needles and metal sounds, replacing them with bouncing pastel clouds, gentle sparkle effects, and a friendly, non-threatening aesthetic that appeals directly to dental-anxious millennials.

# Deliverables
- 1x 30s square explainer video (1:1) for social ads
- 1x 15s responsive landing page loop
- Custom character design sheets for print brochures

# Business Value
- **35% Reduction** in Meta Ad Cost-Per-Acquisition (CPA).
- **68% Increase** in online booking completion rates.
- Unanimous positive feedback from patients citing the "adorable ad" as their reason for booking.
