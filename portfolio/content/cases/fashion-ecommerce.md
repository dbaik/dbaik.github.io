# Client Problem
Maison Nocturne, an independent luxury fashion house, wanted to launch their 'Cosmic Velvet' couture line. They had limited budget for an international runway shoot with multiple locations but needed an asset that looked like a million-dollar high-fashion film to establish prestige and appeal to high-net-worth collectors.

# Creative Idea
We designed 'Liquid Silk & Velvet Dust'—a dreamlike, surreal couture film that takes place in a shifting glass garden. Using advanced AI fabric simulations, we created dresses that shift and ripple in liquid gold and deep purple velvet waves, defying gravity and transforming with every step. The model transitions seamlessly through ethereal, high-prestige backdrops that would be physically impossible or prohibitively expensive to build in real life.

# Deliverables
- 1x 45s landscape brand film (16:9)
- 4x 10s loopable social teasers
- 8x High-fidelity editorial stills for print and social

# Business Value
- **100% Sold Out** of the limited-edition 'Cosmic Velvet' line within 48 hours of video launch.
- **Vimeo Staff Pick** and featured in leading digital design magazines.
- **310% Increase** in organic brand search traffic on Google.
