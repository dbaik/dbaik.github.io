# Client Problem
Ares Athletics, an AI-powered fitness and weightlifting app, wanted to stand out in the saturated App Store. Stock footage of fitness models in gyms was generating low click-through rates. They wanted a video that captured the absolute adrenaline, focus, and raw emotion of hitting a personal record, bridging the gap between gaming culture and fitness.

# Creative Idea
We went with an explosive, high-octane ink-sketch anime style. A runner starts on a dark, wireframe grid (representing raw data) and accelerates, transforming into a glowing cyber-athlete. Fluid, hand-drawn ink splashes and neon electric currents visually depict their energy levels, syncing perfectly with a heavy, syncopated industrial rock soundtrack.

# Deliverables
- 1x 20s vertical YouTube & TikTok commercial (9:16)
- 1x 10s App Store Preview video
- Static high-res promo art assets for social banners

# Business Value
- **+150% Click-Through Rate (CTR)** on YouTube Ads compared to previous campaigns.
- **25% Increase in App Store installs** during the active campaign period.
- **85% Ad View retention rate** (users watching past the 15-second mark).
