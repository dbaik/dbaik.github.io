# Client Problem
Neon Smash, an upscale late-night burger joint, wanted to launch their new 'Volcano Smash' burger to Gen-Z foodies on TikTok and Instagram. Standard food photography felt stale and failed to capture the high-energy, late-night cyberpunk vibe of their physical stores. They needed a hyper-kinetic, futuristic video asset that felt like a movie trailer rather than a menu item.

# Creative Idea
We conceived 'The Neon Sizzle'—a vertical sci-fi odyssey where the burger is forged like a high-tech weapon in a futuristic metropolis. Using AI physics simulation, we designed the ingredients to float and fuse in slow-motion, accompanied by deep-bass synth drops, glowing liquid-cheese rivers, and vaporwave smoke effects.

# Deliverables
- 1x Main 15s vertical cinematic commercial (9:16)
- 3x 6s loopable ambient micro-assets for digital menu boards
- Complete prompt timeline and style guide for secondary marketing campaigns

# Business Value
- **2.4M+ Organic Views** across TikTok and IG Reels in the first 7 days.
- **42% Increase** in late-night foot traffic during the launch weekend.
- **12,000+ Saves** on Instagram, making it the brand's most viral piece of content.
